## Payment methods
Home and motor insurance premiums may be paid by card, bank transfer or monthly auto-debit. Auto-debit is covered under clause 4.2 of the standard payment terms.

## Auto-debit conditions
Auto-debit is collected from an eligible Meridian account on the premium due date. The account must be active and must not carry debit restrictions. A new auto-debit mandate takes up to 5 business days to become active.

## Missed payments
If an auto-debit fails, Meridian will retry once after 3 days and then notify the policyholder. Cover continues during a 15 day grace period.
