## Customers in financial difficulty
Customers who disclose financial hardship must be referred to a trained human specialist. Staff and automated systems must not offer payment holidays or restructuring on their own.

## Vulnerable customers
Any indication that a customer may be pressured, coerced or defrauded must be escalated to the safeguarding team without delay.

## Verification
Identity and KYC checks may only be waived by a compliance officer. They can never be skipped at the customer's request.
