## What is covered
Standard home policies cover fire, theft and accidental damage to the insured property. Exclusions are listed in the policy schedule and always take precedence over general descriptions.

## Flood and natural events
Flood damage is only covered where the policy schedule lists it explicitly. Customers should check clause 3 of their own policy.
